
# 📊 End-to-End Financial Sales Data Analysis

An end-to-end Business Intelligence and Data Analytics project that transforms raw financial sales data into actionable business insights using **Python**, **Power BI**, and **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a financial sales dataset containing **700 sales records** across **5 countries**, **5 business segments**, and **6 products** during **2013–2014**.

The project covers the complete analytics workflow, including:

- Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Business Insight Generation
- Interactive Power BI Dashboard
- Executive Report Preparation

The objective is to identify sales trends, profitability drivers, seasonal patterns, and provide data-driven business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify top-performing countries, products, and customer segments.
- Evaluate the impact of discount strategies on profit.
- Discover monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Develop interactive dashboards for business decision-making.
- Generate actionable recommendations based on data insights.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- 🇺🇸 United States generated the highest sales.
- 🇫🇷 France achieved stronger profitability through balanced product performance and better discount management.
- 🏛️ Government was the highest-performing business segment.
- 🚶 Paseo emerged as the most profitable product.
- 💰 Lower discount levels resulted in higher profit margins.
- 📅 October recorded the highest monthly sales and profit.
- 📈 2014 outperformed 2013 in overall business performance.
